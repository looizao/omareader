# omareader

An offline ebook reader that reads books aloud and shows where the reader is,
word by word, on the page or one word at a time.

## Reading

**RSVP**:
Rapid serial visual presentation: a way of reading where the page is replaced
by one **Flash** at a time, always in the same place, paced by the voice or by
a words-per-minute rate.
_Avoid_: Speed reading, flash reading, word by word

**Flash**:
The word, or short group of words, that **RSVP** shows at one moment.
_Avoid_: Chunk, frame

**Recognition point**:
The letter of a **Flash** pinned to the same spot on screen and tinted, about
a third of the way in, so the eye never has to move between flashes.
_Avoid_: ORP, pivot, focus letter
