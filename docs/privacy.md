# omareader privacy policy

omareader for Android collects no personal data, and shares none.

- The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no accounts of
  its own.
- Dropbox sync is off until you connect your Dropbox account in the app.
  Then the app sends to Dropbox, and only to Dropbox: the books you export
  for the phone, your place in each book, which books you removed, and the
  listening speed and text size. They go to the app's own folder in your
  Dropbox (`Apps/omareader`), which the app is the only one to see, under
  Dropbox's privacy policy. The sign-in is kept encrypted on your phone.
  Disconnecting in the app signs it out; the files stay in your Dropbox
  until you delete them.
- The books you add are copied into the app's private storage on your
  phone, with your reading position. They stay there until you remove a
  book or uninstall the app, and Android backups do not include them.
- The app reads only the files you choose to open with it.

If you install omareader from Google Play, Google handles the download and
updates under its own privacy policy.
